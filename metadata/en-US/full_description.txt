It blocks unwanted calls & SMS messages without replacing your default call/SMS app. 

Filters:

- Contacts	
- Contact Group
- Contact Prefix
- STIR/SHAKEN
- Repeated Calls
- Dialed Number
- Answered Number
- Emergency
- Push Alert
- SMS Alert
- SMS Bomb
- Recent Apps
- Meeting Mode
- Off Time
- Spam Database
- Local AI 
- Schedule & Calendar
- Geolocation & Carrier
- Instant Query
- Report Spam	
- Regex
- and more...
